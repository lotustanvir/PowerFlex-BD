# PowerFlex BD — Data Plan

## Project Scope

Bangladesh-focused AI-powered energy management and Virtual Power Plant platform.

## Energy Sources

1. Solar
2. Wind
3. Hydro
4. Biomass
5. Waste-to-Energy
6. Natural Gas
7. Coal
8. Nuclear

## Core Data

1. Electricity Demand
2. Electricity Generation
3. Power Plant Information
4. Weather
5. Solar Irradiance
6. Wind
7. River / Water Data
8. Waste Data
9. Bangladesh Geographic Data

## AI Modules

1. LoadShield — Electricity Demand Forecasting
2. Solar Forecast
3. Wind Forecast
4. Renewable Energy Potential Prediction
5. PowerFlex Optimizer

## Main Goal

Predict energy demand and renewable generation for Bangladesh
and recommend how available energy resources can be coordinated
to reduce peak-load stress and potential load-shedding.
